League rules

Buy-in

$100 buy-in each year. If you want to leave the league you have to find a suitable replacement to take your team. We'll probably help.

Payouts every year

  • 1st: $700
  • 2nd: $350
  • 3rd: $150

Scoring

Half PPR. 1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE, 1 Flex, 1 Super Flex (can be a QB). No defense or kicker (that's dumb for dynasty and you know it).

Roster

8 bench spots, 3 IR spots, 4 taxi squad spots.

Taxi squad

Rookies who you draft or pick up from waivers before the start of the regular season can be put on your taxi squad. You can activate a player from your taxi squad to your active roster at any point, but once they are activated they cannot go back to your taxi squad. The taxi squad gets cleared out at the end of every regular season and then starts over with the next year's draft.

Trades

You can include future draft picks in trades. If you trade a future 1st round pick you have to pay half of that year's buy-in as a deposit. (E.g. you trade your 2023 1st round pick away: you pay $50 of the buy-in now, and the remaining $50 of the buy-in next year.)

Points Palooza

Every year, weeks 2-5 are Points Palooza. Matchups are manually adjusted after the fact so the six highest-scoring teams each week get a win and the bottom six get a loss. These weeks count toward records but not toward head-to-head history.

Draft

Draft order is the reverse of playoff results (1st place drafts 12th, 2nd drafts 11th, etc.). For non-playoff teams, lowest max points determines draft order. The non-playoff team with the lowest cumulative max points gets the 1st pick in the draft.

Lowest max points

This is calculated every week in the app and shows the most points you could've scored if you played your highest performing players. It takes away the incentive to set a bad lineup when you are tanking. If you want to tank for a better draft pick you have to trade away your good players for future draft picks, as opposed to just putting your good players on your bench or lazily leaving roster spots empty.
