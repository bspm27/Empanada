The case against Schrodinger's Paunch

awillis7: a 2022 startup draft with a manager attached. All figures from Sleeper and KTC, as of Nov 2026.

1. One good draft, four years of coasting

  • 42% of his roster's KTC value comes from the 2022 startup, tied for the most in the league.
  • His top five players (Amon-Ra St. Brown, CeeDee Lamb, James Cook, Lamar Jackson, Christian Watson) are all startup picks he has held since 2022. The 2023 title was won with that core.
  • All five are 27 to 29. The only young player on his roster valued above 4,000 is Colston Loveland.

2. Trades: busy, not good

  • 6 trades, net -88 KTC today.
  • Traded Bo Nix, his own 1.12 pick, for Brian Robinson Jr. Net today: -2,381.
  • Traded George Pickens for Jared Goff (-463). Traded Christian Kirk plus a 2nd for DJ Moore (-514).

3. Drafts: coin flips

  • 2023: ranked 10th of 12 vs. slot value. Dalton Kincaid at 1.08 is now 16th in his class. Stetson Bennett at 4.08.
  • 2026: Ty Simpson at 1.11, 16th in his class.
  • His one great rookie pick, Bo Nix, he then traded away for a running back.

4. Waivers: nothing

  • Five seasons, zero entries in the waiver hall of fame.

5. The lineups

  • 8 losses where the lineup already on his roster would have won.
  • 2022 week 7: started Ryan Tannehill (3.88) over Justin Fields (24.36). Lost 85.7 to 109.4.
  • 2025 week 1: lost 128.6 to 137.6 with 155.1 sitting available.
  • 2025 week 12: lost 110.2 to 123.3 with 137.1 available.
  • 2025 week 2: started Justin Fields (3.98) over Jared Goff (33.96). A 30-point swing.
  • 2025 playoffs: benched Alec Pierce for Christian Watson in week 16 (Pierce 22.6, Watson 2.7). Flipped it in week 17 and started Pierce (0.0) over Watson (19.8). Wrong both times.
  • Started Dalton Schultz for zero points in back-to-back weeks (2022 weeks 4 and 5). Started two zeros in the same week, DeVante Parker and Nico Collins (2022 week 9).
  • Left 240.8 points on the bench in 2022 alone.

6. Shows up, then folds

  • 2022: led the league in points scored and finished outside the top 3.
  • 1 for 3 in championship games, losing the 2024 and 2025 finals.

7. And when the lineup can't win it

  • He tried to rig a first-round bye by convincing his opponent not to set a lineup. When your edge is asking the other guy not to play, that's not a strategy. That's an admission.

Verdict

Take away the 2022 startup draft and what's left is a break-even trader, a coin-flip drafter, a waiver wire ghost, and a guy who starts players on zero. Mid.

League rules

Buy-in

$100 buy-in each year. If you want to leave the league you have to find a suitable replacement to take your team. We'll probably help.

Payouts every year

  • 1st: $700
  • 2nd: $350
  • 3rd: $150

Scoring

Half PPR. 1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE, 1 Flex, 1 Super Flex (can be a QB). No defense or kicker (that's dumb for dynasty and you know it).

Roster

8 bench spots, 3 IR spots, 4 taxi squad spots.

Taxi squad

Rookies who you draft or pick up from waivers before the start of the regular season can be put on your taxi squad. You can activate a player from your taxi squad to your active roster at any point, but once they are activated they cannot go back to your taxi squad. The taxi squad gets cleared out at the end of every regular season and then starts over with the next year's draft.

Trades

You can include future draft picks in trades. If you trade a future 1st round pick you have to pay half of that year's buy-in as a deposit. (E.g. you trade your 2023 1st round pick away: you pay $50 of the buy-in now, and the remaining $50 of the buy-in next year.)

Points Palooza

Every year, weeks 2-4 are Points Palooza. Matchups are manually adjusted after the fact so the six highest-scoring teams each week get a win and the bottom six get a loss. These weeks count toward records but not toward head-to-head history.

Draft

Draft order is the reverse of playoff results (1st place drafts 12th, 2nd drafts 11th, etc.). For non-playoff teams, lowest max points determines draft order. The non-playoff team with the lowest cumulative max points gets the 1st pick in the draft.

Lowest max points

This is calculated every week in the app and shows the most points you could've scored if you played your highest performing players. It takes away the incentive to set a bad lineup when you are tanking. If you want to tank for a better draft pick you have to trade away your good players for future draft picks, as opposed to just putting your good players on your bench or lazily leaving roster spots empty.
